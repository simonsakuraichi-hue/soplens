SOPLens is a documentation and process-clarity aid. It does not replace engineering approval, quality approval, safety review, legal compliance, regulatory review, or the judgment of the responsible process owner.

Generated materials are drafts. Users are responsible for validating all operating limits, hazards, controls, specifications, inspection requirements, escalation paths, records, and release criteria before use in production.

The Founding Process Pack is a limited beta service for one defined process and one revision round. Scope, delivery timing, refund terms, and data-handling details should be confirmed in the checkout terms before accepting paid customers.
