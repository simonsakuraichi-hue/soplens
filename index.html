How it works

From messy reality to reviewable standard work.

Start with the process as it really exists, not the polished version people wish existed.

1. Describe

Paste rough notes, old SOPs, shift handoffs, or a plain-English description.

2. Expose gaps

Identify missing limits, owners, inspection frequency, failure disposition, records, and safety controls.

3. Generate

Create a structured SOP draft, process map, control plan, training checklist, and open-question register.
